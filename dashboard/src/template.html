Data: IPL Complete Dataset 2008–2024 (Kaggle, patrickb1912): 1,095 matches, 260,920 deliveries. Team and ground names standardised with ref_team and ref_venue; super overs excluded.

Rules: win rates use decided matches only (no ties or washouts). Phases: powerplay overs 1–6, middle 7–15, death 16–20. Bowler runs exclude byes and leg-byes. Player impact uses the formulas in sql/07_player_impact.sql.
